Division, Differentiation & Gene Expression

Use this model to show how one cell can produce many cells, how cells with the same DNA become different cell types by switching different genes on, and how division plus differentiation maintain tissues.

Part 4: Use the model to explain

Prompt: How do cell division, differentiation, and gene expression help produce and maintain a complex organism?

A strong response mentions division, differentiation, same DNA, different genes switched on, specialized tissues, and repair/maintenance.
Teacher note: Keeps the activity touch-friendly while making the central, misconception-resistant idea interactive: most specialized cells contain the same DNA, and different genes are switched on. Pairs with the organization model — differentiation here ends where the tissue/organ hierarchy begins.
